How much does hardwood floor installation cost in Maryland?
+

Hardwood installation in Maryland typically runs $6–$12 per square foot for materials + labor, depending on species, subfloor condition, and complexity. We provide free in-home estimates with exact pricing — no vague ranges.

How long does hardwood refinishing take?
+

Most refinishing projects take 3–4 days: 1 day sanding, 1 day staining, 1–2 days for finish coats to cure. We minimize disruption and give you a day-by-day schedule before we start.

Is refinishing cheaper than replacing floors?
+

Yes — typically 50–75% less. Most hardwood floors can be refinished 4–6 times over their lifetime. We'll give you an honest assessment and tell you if replacement actually makes more sense for your situation.

Are you licensed and insured?
+

Yes. USA Pro Floors LLC is fully licensed, bonded, and insured in both Maryland and Washington D.C. We carry full liability insurance and workers' compensation on every single job. You're always protected. Verify our Maryland MHIC license or learn more about wood flooring methods on Wikipedia.

Do you offer free estimates?
+

Absolutely. We come to your home, assess the floors in person, and provide a detailed written estimate — completely free with zero obligation. Most estimates are scheduled within 24–48 hours.

What is hardwood floor sanding and finishing?
+

Hardwood floor sanding and finishing is the process of sanding your hardwood floors down to bare wood, applying a custom stain, and sealing with a durable oil-based or water-based finish. It's the core of every hardwood floor refinishing project and brings scratched, faded, or worn hardwood floors back to life at a fraction of replacement cost.

Do you install porcelain and ceramic tile?
+

Yes. We install porcelain tile, ceramic tile, large-format tile, subway tile, and mosaics in kitchens, bathrooms, foyers, mudrooms, showers, and commercial spaces. Straight lines, tight grout, proper backer board, and lasting results — residential and commercial throughout Maryland, DC, and Virginia.

Do you install marble and natural stone?
+

Yes. We install marble, granite, travertine, slate, limestone, and other natural stone for flooring, entryways, bathrooms, and fireplaces. Honed, polished, or leathered finishes available. Every stone installation is properly sealed to protect against stains and etching.

Do you install carpet?
+

Yes — we install plush, berber, frieze, and commercial-grade carpet for homes, offices, stairs, and rental properties. Full padding, tack strip, seamless seams, and furniture moving included on every carpet installation job across Maryland, DC, and Virginia.

What is dustless hardwood refinishing?
+

Dustless hardwood refinishing uses HEPA-filter sanders connected to containment vacuums to capture 99%+ of sanding dust at the source. Your furniture, HVAC, and lungs stay clean. We offer dustless hardwood floor refinishing as a standard option on every refinishing project.

How often can hardwood floors be refinished?
+

Solid hardwood floors can typically be refinished 4–6 times over their lifetime (roughly every 10–15 years, depending on traffic). Engineered hardwood with a thick wear layer can usually be refinished 1–3 times. We measure your existing floor before quoting to confirm how much wood is left to sand.

How much does hardwood floor refinishing cost in DC, VA and Maryland?
+

Hardwood floor refinishing in DC, Virginia, and Maryland typically runs $2.50–$4 per square foot for a standard sand + stain + finish. Custom stains, water-based finishes, and dustless upgrades add a bit more. We give you an exact written quote after a free in-home inspection — no vague ranges.
